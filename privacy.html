Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Purpose

This is a personal, non-commercial OAuth application used by the account owner to access Google Drive through rclone.

Google Drive Data

The application authorizes rclone to access Google Drive files and metadata for personal file management, synchronization, and backup operations.

Data Storage

OAuth credentials are maintained in the owner's local rclone configuration. Files may be accessed, copied, or backed up between the owner's devices and Google Drive.

Data Sharing

This OAuth integration is intended solely for the account owner's use. It does not operate a separate service that collects or sells Google user data.

Security

Access is controlled through Google's OAuth authorization process and the owner's local computer security controls.

Revoking Access

The account owner can revoke the application's access through Google Account security settings.

Contact

Questions about this personal integration can be directed to the contact address shown on its Google OAuth consent screen.
